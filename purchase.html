Important — Verify Your Device Before Ordering

Before placing an order, install and open the Canopy Gap Android application on the phone you intend to use. Grant the required camera and location permissions, then wait until the Start button becomes enabled. This confirms that the required permissions, camera preview, accelerometer, and magnetic-field (compass) sensor are available. Tap Start, then select the purchase link on the in-app activation screen to order your activation code.

Please order from inside the Android app only after the Start button is enabled. This check helps you avoid paying before confirming that your device meets the application's basic requirements.
